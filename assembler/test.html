Morae

・ marks a mora boundary. A long vowel marked ː still spans two rhythmic morae. Small っ occupies a beat of its own as a held consonant closure.

Sung IPA

This is broad IPA for singing. Vowels are kept audible even where ordinary speech may devoice them. The recording may restore, shorten, or color individual vowels differently.

Key sounds

[ɾ] is a brief Japanese tap, [ɕi] is し, [tɕi] is ち, [tsɯ] is つ, and [ɯ] is an unrounded Japanese う.
